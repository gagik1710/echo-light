Privacy Policy

Echo: Light · last updated November 15, 2026

Echo: Light ("the game") is made by Gagik Khalatyan ("I", "me"). This policy explains what data the game uses.

What I collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no login and no servers of its own. Your progress, settings and scores are stored only on your device. I do not receive, sell or share them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use data such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking), IP address, approximate location, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the game asks for your consent through Google's consent form (UMP) before ads are personalised. You can change your choice at any time in the game under Settings → Privacy & Ads (shown where required).

On iPhone you can decline tracking when the system asks, or change it later in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Without tracking, ads are still shown but are less relevant.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because I do not hold personal data about you, there is nothing for me to access or delete. For data held by Google, use Google's own tools and privacy controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
