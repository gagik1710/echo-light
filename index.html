Support

Found a bug, have a question or an idea? Open an issue on the support page and describe what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version. I read every report.

Frequently asked

  • Is my progress saved? Yes, it is stored on your device and included in your normal iCloud/device backup. There is no account.
  • How do I change my ad choices? In the game: Settings → Privacy & Ads (shown where required). On iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • The ad did not give a reward. The reward is granted only after the ad is watched and closed. If it still fails, report it using the link above.
